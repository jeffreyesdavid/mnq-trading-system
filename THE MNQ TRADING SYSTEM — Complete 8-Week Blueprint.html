Part 1: Trading Fundamentals

What Are Futures Contracts?

A futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell something at a specific price on a specific date. For you: you don't own the Nasdaq-100 index. You control a contract that tracks it. Small price movements = big profit/loss for you (leverage).

Key Differences from Stocks: No holding overnight, guaranteed liquidity, small capital needed, leverage built in.

How MNQ Works

Contract Specifications:

  • 1 point movement = $2 profit/loss per contract
  • Minimum tick = 0.25 points = $0.50 per contract
  • Trading hours: 6:00pm - 5:00pm ET (trade regular hours 9:30am - 4:00pm ET)
  • You control ~$105,000 of notional value with $50 risk per contract

Example: You buy 1 MNQ at 4200.00. Price goes to 4205.00 (5 points). Profit: 5 × $2 × 1 = $10.

Part 2: The 1R Risk Framework

What Is 1R?

Definition: 1R = the amount of money you're willing to lose on a single trade.

Starting 1R: $50. Why? On a $25K account, losing $50 is -0.2%. Losing $50 × 3 trades max = -$150/day = -0.6% per day. Sustainable.

Calculating Position Size

Formula: Contracts = Risk Dollars ÷ (Stop Loss Distance in Points × $2 per point)

Example: Entry 4200, Stop 4195 (5 points), 1R = $50. Contracts = $50 ÷ (5 × $2) = $50 ÷ $10 = 5 contracts.

Verify: 5 contracts × 5 points × $2 = $50 loss if stopped out. Perfect 1R.

Daily, Weekly, Monthly Limits

Daily Loss Limit (3R): 3 × $50 = $150. Hit -$150? Stop immediately, rest of day.

Weekly Loss Limit (6R): 6 × $50 = $300. Hit -$300? Stop for the week.

Why This Works: Consistent small wins compound. One big loss destroys everything. This framework protects you.

Part 3: Setup Building

What Is a Setup?

A setup is a repeatable pattern in price action where you have an edge. Your edge = your ability to predict what happens next better than random guessing (50%).

NOT a Setup: "The price is going up, I feel it" (intuition). "Everyone on Twitter is bullish" (social proof).

IS a Setup: "Price touches support 3 times, bounces 2 times, then hits it again = 80%+ bounce chance" (documented edge).

Three High-Probability Setups

Setup 1: Support/Resistance Bounce

How to Identify: Find a price level where MNQ bounced 2+ times. Wait for price to approach that level again. Enter when it bounces off.

Example: MNQ bounced at 4200 twice. Now at 4205, falling toward 4200. Entry: 4202. Stop: 4198 (4 points). Target: 4210 (8 points). Risk/Reward: 1:2 (good).

Win Rate: 55-60% if bounce is clean.

Setup 2: Breakout + Pullback

How to Identify: Price trapped between support/resistance. Breaks above/below. Comes back (pullback). Enter on pullback.

Example: MNQ between 4190-4210 (range). Breaks above 4210. Comes back to 4210 (pullback). Entry: 4212. Stop: 4208 (4 points). Target: 4225 (13 points). Risk/Reward: 1:3 (excellent).

Win Rate: 60-65% if range is established.

Setup 3: Moving Average Bounce

How to Identify: Add 20-period MA. Price is above/below MA (in trend). Price pulls back to MA. MA is sloping in trend direction. Enter when price bounces off MA.

Example: MNQ in uptrend (each bounce higher). 20 MA rising. Price pulls back to MA at 4205. Entry: 4206. Stop: 4203 (3 points). Target: 4215 (9 points). Risk/Reward: 1:3 (excellent).

Win Rate: 55-60% if trend is strong.

Finding Your Own Setups

Process: Trade one setup for 2 weeks. Review 40 trades. Which ones won most? Screenshot winners. What do they have in common? Write description: "My edge is X setup at Y level in Z direction."

Part 4: A+ / A / B / C Grading System

Why Grade?

You can win on a C setup and lose on an A+ setup. But over 100 trades, A+ setups compound while C setups destroy accounts. Grade every setup independently from P&L.

A+ Setup (Textbook)

2+ confluences, high conviction, risk/reward 1:2+, win rate 60%+. Trade with 2 contracts.

A Setup (Good)

1-2 confluences, risk/reward 1:1.5-1:2, win rate 55-58%. Trade with 1-2 contracts.

B Setup (Okay but Risky)

1 weak confluence, risk/reward 1:1 or worse, win rate 50-53%. Trade 0-1 contracts or skip.

C Setup (Avoid)

No clear confluence, desperation, revenge, win rate 45-50%. Skip completely.

Hard Truth: If you trade a C setup and win, you learn bad lessons. If you lose, you lose money. Skip them.

Part 5: Psychology Framework

Fear (Most Common Pre-Evaluation)

What It Looks Like: You see perfect A+ setup, hesitate. Enter smaller size. Exit early. Avoid volatile times.

How to Overcome: Remember: You're risking $50 on $25,000. That's 0.2%. Over 20 trades at 55% win rate, you make $100 net. Fear is telling you something is dangerous, but losing $50 is data, not danger.

Drill: Trade micro sizes (1 contract, $10-20 risk) for 5 days. Lose all 5 if you have to. Confidence builds.

Greed (Most Common Post-Evaluation)

What It Looks Like: Pass eval Week 3, get payout Week 5. Suddenly want $5,000/week. Jump from 2 to 5 contracts. One bad trade = -$500. Account underwater. Panic. Account blown.

How to Overcome: Write this down: "Greed blows accounts. Discipline compounds." Same size Week 5 as Week 4. Same daily targets Week 5. Only scale Week 7 after 4 weeks of consistency.

Drill: Week 5-6: Same size as Week 4 (no exceptions). Only scale Week 7 if 60%+ A+ both weeks.

Revenge Trading (Killer)

What It Looks Like: Lose trade 1 (-$50). Immediately re-enter to get it back. Lose again (-$75). Now down -$125 and angry. Third trade (-$100). Account -$225, only 3 trades morning.

How to Overcome: Rule: Lose once → wait 2 hours. Lose twice → wait 4 hours. Use this time to calm down. You'll save yourself from -$225 to -$75.

Overconfidence (Killer of Consistency)

What It Looks Like: Great week (+$2,000), feel like genius. Skip pre-market routine. Trade B/C setups. Next week -$1,500. Account back to where you started.

How to Overcome: Reminder: The system made the money, not you. Same pre-market ritual after good weeks. Same grade filtering (A+ only). Document: "All my big losses came after overconfident weeks."

Part 6: Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Improper Position Sizing

The Error: You see A+ setup, get excited, trade 5 contracts instead of 2. Setup works, make $100. Think "let's do it again." Next setup doesn't work, lose -$100. Your risk/reward is now 1:1 (coin flip).

How to Avoid: Every trade: Calculate contracts from stop loss. Formula: Contracts = Risk ÷ (Stop Points × $2). Write it down. Verify your loss equals your 1R.

Mistake 2: No Pre-Planned Stop Loss

The Error: Enter at 4210. "I'll set stop if it goes below 4207." Price immediately drops to 4208. Wait for bounce. Price keeps dropping. Finally hit stop at 4195 (15 points instead of 3). Risk was $50. Loss was $150.

How to Avoid: RULE: Set stop BEFORE entering. Write it in journal before clicking enter. Never move stop farther into loss.

Mistake 3: Holding for the Home Run

The Error: Entry 4210, Stop 4207, Target 4220. Price reaches 4220, think "let me hold for 4225." Price goes 4220 → 4215 → hit stop. Profit turned into loss.

How to Avoid: Your target is your exit. Exit at target. Period. You can scale out (sell half at 4220, hold half to 4225). But full position out at target = rule.

Mistake 4: Over-Trading

The Error: Trade 1 (win +$40) → Trade 2 (B-grade, lose -$50) → Trade 3 (C-grade revenge, lose -$50) → 3 trades, 1 win, 2 losses, net -$60.

How to Avoid: Max 3 trades/day (realistically 1-2). If you hit daily target ($300), stop. Don't stack profits.

Mistake 5: Ignoring Loss Limits

The Error: Daily limit -$150. Trade 1: -$60. Trade 2: -$50 (-$110 cumulative). "I can take one more." Trade 3: -$60 (-$170, over limit). You broke your own rule.

How to Avoid: Treat -$150 as non-negotiable. When you hit -$100, you know you have one more trade. When you hit -$150, you MUST stop.

Mistake 6: No Trade Journal

The Error: Trade 30 times Week 1. Remember you won 15, lost 15. Think "my setups are 50/50" and quit.

Reality (if you journaled): 12 A+ setups: 9 wins (75%). 10 B setups: 5 wins (50%). 8 C setups: 2 wins (25%). Your edge is HUGE, but you're trading too many B/C.

How to Avoid: Journal every trade (30 seconds). Review after 20 trades. Pattern emerges. You see your edge.

Mistake 7: Trading Bad Times

The Error: Trade first 30 min (9:30-10:00) and last 30 min (3:30-4:00). Highly volatile. Stops hit more often. Win rate drops 60% → 40%.

How to Avoid: No trades 9:30-10:00 and 3:30-4:00. Sweet spot: 10:00-3:30 (5.5 hours steady trading). You'll still get 3-5 quality setups. Higher win rate, less stress.

Part 9: FAQ

Can I trade part-time while working?

Answer: Yes, if disciplined. Morning session 9:30am-1:00pm (3.5 hours). Before work or lunch break. Set targets, stop, journal at end of day.

How much can I make per month?

Answer: Depends on consistency and size. Conservative (1-2 contracts): $6K-8K/month. Moderate (2-3): $8K-12K. Aggressive (4-5): $12K-20K. But only after months of consistency, 60%+ win rate, slow scaling.

What if I fail my evaluation?

Answer: Review your journal. Find the pattern. Did you break rules? Win rate suck? Overtrade? Fix one thing, re-apply (usually resets 30 days).

When should I go full-time?

Answer: After 6 months consistent profit. Account $50K+, 55%+ win rate, 12 months living expenses saved. Don't rush.
